Your own secret code

Marlek turns what you write in Spanish into a secret code made of emoji and symbols — and translates it right back. For sending secret messages between friends and siblings.

Download on the App Store How it works

For iPhone and iPad · now on the App Store

What is Marlek?

Marlek is a cipher language: every letter, number, or symbol is swapped for another character or emoji, following a fixed table. Its creator invented it with pen and paper; this app brings it to your iPhone and iPad.

What you can do

🔁

Translator

Type in Spanish and watch the Marlek appear instantly. Or the other way around: paste a secret message and read it in Spanish. It works both ways, and with Siri, Shortcuts and Control Center too.

⌨️

Marlek keyboard

A system keyboard: turn it on and write Marlek inside any app — messages, notes, anywhere. No Full Access required.

📖

Key table

The full key sheet, always within reach. Every letter and symbol with its Marlek equivalent, to learn or look up.

🎯

Practice

Learn the code by playing, without the key sheet: Time Attack, Write the Letter, Decode the Message and Choose the Answer. With a day streak and ranks, from Initiate to Marlek Master.

🃏

Share cards

Turn a short message or your rank into a card, in story or square format, for your chats and stories.

💬

My Messages

Save the messages you want to see again, give them a title and search them. If you use iCloud, you see them on all your devices.

Made with care

🔐

Truly private

It translates on your device. What you save stays there and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud. No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Marlek collects no data. Read the privacy policy.

Apple-native

Built for iPhone and iPad with Apple's Liquid Glass design. Fast, clear, and right at home on your device.

Kid & family friendly

Made for sending secret messages between friends and siblings. Playful, Spanish-first, easy to use.
